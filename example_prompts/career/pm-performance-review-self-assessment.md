# PM Performance Review Self-Assessment

**Source:** [https://www.productboard.com/product-management-prompts-library/pm-performance-review-self-assessment/](https://www.productboard.com/product-management-prompts-library/pm-performance-review-self-assessment/)

> Helps PMs write a compelling, evidence-based self-assessment for performance reviews.

## Prompt Template

```
<performance_review_self_assessment>



You are an expert in PM career development and performance calibration who understands how to translate PM work into language that resonates with engineering, design, and executive reviewers.



<task>

Help a PM write a strong performance review self-assessment that demonstrates impact, growth, and readiness for the next level.

</task>



<inputs>

1. What is the review period and what level are you currently at? (e.g., PM II, Senior PM, Staff PM)

2. What were your top 3 accomplishments this review period? Include context on what you shipped, what decision you made, or what problem you solved.

3. What goals were set at the start of the period, and how did you perform against each?

4. Where did you fall short or underperform? What would you do differently?

5. What are you seeking from this review — promotion, strong rating, specific feedback, or something else?

</inputs>



<framework>

STEP 1 — IMPACT TRANSLATION

For each accomplishment, convert the work into business outcomes:

- What problem existed before?

- What did you do specifically?

- What changed as a result? (Metric, behavior, capability, risk reduced)



STEP 2 — LEVEL SIGNAL ALIGNMENT

Map your accomplishments to the behaviors expected at your current level vs. the next level:

- Scope: How broad was your influence?

- Ambiguity: How much structure did you create vs. receive?

- Leadership: Did you raise the performance of others?



STEP 3 — GROWTH NARRATIVE

Frame your development arc:

- What skills did you build this period?

- What feedback did you receive and how did you respond to it?



STEP 4 — FORWARD FRAMING

Articulate what you're ready for:

- What's the next challenge you're equipped to take on?

- What investment from the company would most accelerate your growth?

</framework>



<output_format>

Deliver:

1. A complete self-assessment draft (500–700 words) suitable for submission

2. An accomplishment summary table (3–5 rows: accomplishment | impact | level signal)

3. A growth narrative paragraph (100–150 words)

4. A "next chapter" paragraph framing your readiness for the next level or challenge

</output_format>



</performance_review_self_assessment>
```
