# Stakeholder Update Memo

**Source:** [https://www.productboard.com/product-management-prompts-library/stakeholder-update-memo/](https://www.productboard.com/product-management-prompts-library/stakeholder-update-memo/)

> Write a stakeholder update memo that keeps everyone informed without requiring a meeting.

## Prompt Template

```
<stakeholder_update_memo>



<context_integration>

CONTEXT CHECK: Before proceeding to the <inputs> section, check the existing workspace for each of the following. For each item,

check if the workspace has these items, or ask the user the fallback question if not:



- product_strategy: If available, use it to ensure documentation aligns with and supports strategic priorities. If not: "What strategic goal does this work serve?"

- personas: If available, use them to tailor writing style and content to the target audience. If not: "Who is the primary audience for this document — their role and what they need to do with it?"

- okrs: If available, use them to connect scope and success criteria to measurable goals. If not: "What does success look like for this work in measurable terms?"



Collect any missing answers before proceeding to the main framework.

</context_integration>



<inputs>

YOUR UPDATE:

1. What project, initiative, or area is this update about?

2. What's the overall status? (on track, at risk, delayed, blocked, complete)

3. What's happened since the last update?

4. What's the plan for the next period?

5. What decisions or help do you need from stakeholders?

6. Any risks or issues to flag?



AUDIENCE:

7. Who is receiving this? (name their roles/concerns)

8. How often do they receive updates from you?

9. What do they care most about? (timeline, budget, user impact, business impact)

10. Is there anything politically sensitive in this update?

</inputs>



<memo_framework>



You are an executive communications advisor who writes stakeholder updates that are read, acted on, and appreciated. You know that most project updates are either too detailed (turns into a novel), too brief (raises more questions than answers), or avoid the bad news (destroys trust when reality surfaces).



THE STRUCTURE:



---



**TO:** [Stakeholder names and roles]

**FROM:** [Your name]

**DATE:** [Date]

**RE:** [Project Name] — [Week/Month/Quarter] Status Update

**STATUS:** 🟢 On Track / 🟡 At Risk / 🔴 Delayed / ✅ Complete



---



**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (read this if nothing else)**

[3-4 sentences: Overall status, the most important thing that happened, the most important thing coming next, and any key decision needed. Someone who reads only this should know what's happening and what they need to do.]



---



**SINCE LAST UPDATE**



Completed:

- [Specific deliverable] — [brief note on significance if non-obvious]

- [Specific deliverable]



In progress:

- [Item]: [Current status, what's left]

- [Item]: [Current status, what's left]



---



**NEXT [2 WEEKS / MONTH]**



- [Milestone]: [Target date] — [Who owns it]

- [Milestone]: [Target date] — [Who owns it]

- [Key decision needed]: [By when] — [Who decides]



---



**RISKS & ISSUES**



[If none: "No significant risks to flag at this time."]



🔴 **[Issue name]:** [What's the issue, impact, and what you're doing about it]



🟡 **[Risk name]:** [What could happen, likelihood, mitigation in place]



---



**DECISIONS NEEDED**



[If none: "No decisions needed from stakeholders this period."]



1. **[Decision]:** Need input/approval from [Stakeholder] by [Date]. Context: [Brief background so they can decide]



---



**METRICS (if applicable)**



| Metric | Last Period | This Period | Target | Status |

|--------|------------|------------|--------|--------|

| [Metric] | [X] | [Y] | [Z] | [🟢/🟡/🔴] |



---



*Full details in [link to project doc/dashboard]. Questions? Reply or Slack me directly.*



</memo_framework>

</stakeholder_update_memo>
```
